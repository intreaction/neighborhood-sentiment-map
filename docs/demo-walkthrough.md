# Demo walkthrough

1. Open `web/index.html` (or the local preview). Explain the question: how did nearby
   online review activity change around a public investment, and what do the reviews
   say about the surrounding experience?
2. Start with Lafitte. Show the primary outcome, raw near/comparison counts and
   expected-versus-observed calculation. Explain the units: growth-adjusted excess
   Yelp reviews per $1 million of reported cost, over two post-opening years.
3. Inspect the footprint and its coverage note. Review the text evidence, topic
   language and provenance. Separate coverage from classification accuracy.
4. Select Sun Link to show a negative comparison-adjusted outcome. This does not
   establish project failure. Select Water Works Park to show why low baseline
   support withholds an estimate instead of inventing one.
5. Use Dilworth Park as a proposal example. Its historical inputs demonstrate the
   workflow; they are not measurements of a new location. Change cost from $55M to
   $80M and inspect the estimate, error span, comparables and timing sensitivity.
   The fitted association is not the causal return from increasing spending.
6. Select your own baseline profile, or import a documented local JSON profile.
   Explain the 500 m catchment, two-year baseline, reviewed-business denominator,
   extraction source/date, supported input range and assumption-only label.
7. Export the analysis record. The JSON preserves inputs, source metadata, model
   and evidence versions, support decisions and the resulting estimate.
8. Close on the substantive research finding: richer text can improve understanding
   of a place without necessarily improving this small-sample CE forecast. Keep
   advanced text challenger results and held-out errors visible.

Do not present this prototype as a financial ROI model, an estimate of public benefit,
or a causal funding recommendation. It is an evidence explorer and experimental
scenario model. Five geometry proxies, nominal mixed-scope costs, historical Yelp
coverage and unvalidated rule labels remain material limitations.

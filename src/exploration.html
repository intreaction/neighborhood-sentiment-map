These are project-defined mapped study areas, not official city or metro populations. Population coverage uses the current minimum-population setting; zero and missing populations are always excluded. Regional sentiment is review-weighted across those same covered ZCTAs that meet the review threshold in the selected quarter. Funding rates are not amounts received by individual residents.

Population is the Census TIGERweb 2020 ZCTA population count (POP100), held fixed throughout 2012–2021. This supports a comparison of scale; it does not estimate historical per-capita spending. Tiny residential populations can produce extreme rates. An office ZIP can record funding that benefits a much larger region.

03 / Inside the reviews

What are people talking about?

Mentions, not conclusions

Share of all reviews in this area and quarter. Select a theme to filter sentiment throughout the page.

Rules search for words and phrases, and a review may match several themes. A cleanliness mention can be praise or a complaint; it may refer to a bathroom, not a street. Theme sentiment is the score of the whole review, not the sentiment of that specific aspect.

Look beyond the average

Star ratings for all reviews in the selected area and quarter, regardless of theme.

Stars and text sentiment measure different things. Yelp reviews are self-selected, and business or reviewer composition can change over time.

Read the language behind a tag

Short excerpts from a seeded sample, balanced across whole-review sentiment classes within each city, year and theme. They illustrate matching language and are not a representative estimate. Scores apply to the full review. No reviewer identities are included.

The next analytical step

Validate the tags before interpreting them.

Human-label reviews across cities and years, measure precision and recall for each theme, and distinguish business interiors from the surrounding area. Then compare these transparent rules with a text classifier. These exploratory tags give us a testable starting point.

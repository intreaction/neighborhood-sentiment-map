Earlier methods, preserved for comparison.

Archived methods: these calculations retain the earlier registry and windows, including the former Gateway Arch and Crescent Park milestones. They are sensitivity references, not the current primary project results. Open current project analyses. Capital Efficiency (CE) divides a review difference by reported project cost in $ millions. Four original projects use matched business pairs. Sun Link now uses all 717 Yelp listings within 500 m of its full route: its expected post-period reviews equal corridor baseline reviews multiplied by the 1.5–8 km Tucson comparison area's review-growth factor. The six expansion projects use unmatched near and farther cohort means. These methods produce different comparisons, so scores across methods are not directly comparable. All values are descriptive, not causal returns.

04 / Inside the reviews

What are people talking about?

Overall sentiment mixes many experiences. Separate reviews mentioning parking or surroundings from reviews about service, price and products, then carry that lens back to the map.

Selected quarter · overlapping text tags
Text categories have not been built. Run the review-topic pipeline to enable this section.

Mentions, not conclusions

Share of all reviews in this area and quarter. Select a theme to filter review activity throughout the page.

Rules search for words and phrases, and a review may match several themes. A cleanliness mention can be praise or a complaint; it may refer to a bathroom, not a street. Theme sentiment is the score of the whole review, not the sentiment of that specific aspect.

Look beyond the average

Star ratings for all reviews in the selected area and quarter, regardless of theme.

Stars and text sentiment measure different things. Yelp reviews are self-selected, and business or reviewer composition can change over time.

Read the language behind a tag

Short excerpts from a seeded sample, balanced across whole-review sentiment classes within each city, year and theme. They illustrate matching language and are not a representative estimate. Scores apply to the full review. No reviewer identities are included.

The next analytical step

Validate the tags before interpreting them.

Human-label reviews across cities and years, measure precision and recall for each theme, and distinguish business interiors from the surrounding area. Then compare these transparent rules with a text classifier. These exploratory tags give us a testable starting point.

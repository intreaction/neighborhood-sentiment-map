__EXPLORATION__
About the data & how to read this map

This atlas explores associations between federal funding and business-review sentiment, not whether funding caused a change. Yelp measures experiences with businesses, not a representative survey of neighborhood well-being. Reviews cover full quarters from 2012 through 2021 in the January 2022 dataset.

City views are project-defined ZIP groups. Map polygons are Census ZCTAs; some postal ZIPs have no polygon. City aggregates include those ZIPs. ZIP sentiment defaults to at least 30 reviews per quarter, adjustable from 10 to 200; annual change compares the selected quarter with the same quarter one year earlier and requires the selected minimum review count in both. City sentiment is review-weighted. Map sentiment colors show relative standing against a fixed five-city median, not positive/negative classes.

Funding is the existing project's place-based assistance panel: recorded place-of-performance obligations, including negative adjustments, not expenditures or completed projects. A recorded ZIP is not a verified benefit footprint. Funding circles sit at area centers and represent positive net obligations only. Zero means no net amount in this selected extract, not no public investment. The funding panel's classification and provenance still require reconciliation with the EDA before inferential claims.

Geometry and context: Census TIGERweb. Review scores: VADER. All map and chart data are embedded for offline use. ZIPs and ZCTAs · USAspending · Yelp dataset

__EXPLORATION__
About the data & how to read this map

This atlas maps project capital efficiency alongside Yelp review activity, recorded federal funding and documented project footprints. Review counts are a proxy for online engagement, not visits, revenue, resident well-being, or an effect caused by investment. Reviews cover full quarters from 2012 through 2021 in the January 2022 dataset.

The default map colors ZIPs by quarterly Yelp review count on a fixed logarithmic scale; moving the timeline slider updates those colors. The Capital Efficiency (CE) badge shows a two-year before/after review difference per $1M of reported project cost. Four original projects use matched business samples. Sun Link uses all 717 Yelp listings within 500 m of its full route, adjusted by review growth in a farther 1.5–8 km Tucson comparison area; this is an unmatched comparison. It is pinned to the project post-window on the timeline, remains fixed while the quarter slider moves, and is descriptive. Gray means insufficient support or no estimate. Cost figures can mix public and private funding. Radius and income filters change the available project cohort and score; Sun Link income subgroups are available only at 500 m. City views are project-defined ZIP groups. Map polygons are Census ZCTAs; some postal ZIPs have no polygon. City aggregates include those ZIPs. Map growth compares review counts with the same quarter one year earlier. Both quarters must meet the selected minimum review count (30 by default, adjustable from 10 to 200). The color scale is fixed and clips growth below −50% or above +100%; the count view uses a fixed logarithmic color scale. Project outlines show documented footprints, while the five-case benchmark below compares nearby businesses with farther businesses (500 meters by default). ZIP colors in the other map modes do not represent those project catchments.

Funding is the existing project's place-based assistance panel: recorded place-of-performance obligations, including negative adjustments, not expenditures or completed projects. A recorded ZIP is not a verified benefit footprint. Funding circles sit at area centers and represent positive net obligations only; they are separate from the outlined project footprints. Zero means no net amount in this selected extract, not no public investment. The funding panel's classification and provenance still require reconciliation with the EDA before inferential claims.

Geometry and context: Census TIGERweb. Review scores: VADER. All map and chart data are embedded for offline use. ZIPs and ZCTAs · USAspending · Yelp dataset

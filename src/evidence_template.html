How to read capital efficiency

An activity proxy,
with its assumptions visible.

The primary measure is growth-adjusted net Yelp reviews per $1 million of reported project cost, using two baseline and two post-opening years. The opening year is omitted.

Expected nearby post reviews = nearby baseline reviews × farther-area growth factor
CE = (observed nearby post − expected nearby post) ÷ cost in $M

The nearby catchment is within 500 m of the mapped footprint or proxy center; the comparison is 1.5–8 km away. A negative value means nearby review activity grew more slowly than the comparison. It does not establish project failure or a causal effect.

Text signals use provisional clause-level VADER polarity and barrier rules. Negative topic counts require an explicit area target; all three text-feature shares use all baseline reviews as their denominator. They can misread sarcasm, mixed experiences, and whether a comment describes the neighborhood. Extraction coverage is distinct from label accuracy.

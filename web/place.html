How much trust to place in the estimate

Predictions versus historical outcomes

Each prediction holds out the project’s entire city during training. Points near the diagonal were predicted accurately; points far away were not.

What these regressions can tell us

The selected ridge regression relates the two-year outcome to budget, reviewed-business count, and review intensity. Historical trend lines fit annual review activity before opening using ordinary least squares; they describe past patterns. A reliable forecast of the timing of uplift would require a validated time model and more observations around each opening. Visits, sales, foot traffic, and sentiment changes are not predicted here.
